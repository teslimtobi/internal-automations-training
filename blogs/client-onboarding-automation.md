# Client onboarding automation: no more lost handoffs

A new client signs, everyone is pleased, and then nothing happens for a week. Nobody meant for that to happen. The contract landed in one inbox, the person who sets up the project folder was never told, and the welcome email waited for someone to remember it. Client onboarding automation fixes this by turning those handoffs into steps that run on their own. In this article we look at where manual onboarding breaks, what an automated onboarding workflow looks like in Microsoft 365, and why most firms get better results with a partner than by building it alone.

## Where manual onboarding breaks

Most onboarding problems are not about effort. They come from handoffs that depend on memory. One person has to tell the next person, and if they are busy or off sick, the chain stops.

The worst part is that nobody sees it stop. There is no alert when a folder is never created or a kickoff form sits unread. The client simply waits, and the first sign of trouble is often a polite chaser from them.

## How Appnable automates onboarding in Microsoft 365

Appnable builds onboarding flows on the tools your team already uses. When a signed contract arrives in Outlook, a flow can create the SharePoint folder, open a Teams channel and send the welcome email with the kickoff form attached.

Each step has a named owner and a clear trigger. If something fails, the right person gets a message straight away instead of finding out days later. Microsoft's own [Power Automate documentation](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/power-automate/) shows the building blocks, and we shape them around how your firm really works.

## Where DIY onboarding automation stalls

Plenty of teams try to organize this themselves. The first flow usually works. The trouble starts when the process changes, the person who built it moves on, or nobody knows which flow sends which email.

A partner brings a written map of the process, tested flows and someone to call when a step breaks. That keeps the automation running long after the first launch.

## Final thoughts

Late onboarding rarely comes from one big mistake. It comes from small handoffs that nobody owns. Mapping the process, giving every step an owner and letting automation handle the routine parts means new clients hear from you on day one, not day nine. If your onboarding still relies on someone remembering, it is worth a closer look. Book a call with our team to talk it through.

## FAQs

### What is client onboarding automation?

It is the use of software to run the routine steps after a client signs, such as creating folders, sending welcome emails and updating trackers. Each step starts automatically when the one before it finishes, so nothing depends on memory. See more answers on our [FAQ page](https://www.appnable.com/faq/).

Yes. Power Automate can watch an Outlook inbox, create SharePoint folders, post in Teams and send emails without code. Most firms still need help mapping the process and testing each flow before it goes live. Find out more on our [FAQ page](https://www.appnable.com/faq/).
