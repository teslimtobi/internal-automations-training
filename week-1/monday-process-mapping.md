# Week 1 - Monday Checkpoint: Process Mapping Foundations

## The 4 Parts of Every Workflow

1. Trigger
   - Definition: The initial event or input that kicks off the process.
   - Purpose: Establishes when and why the system begins execution.

2. Steps
   - Definition: The sequential actions, tasks, and data handoffs performed.
   - Purpose: Moves the work from its raw starting state toward completion.

3. Decision Points
   - Definition: Logic branches where rules evaluate data and determine the path taken.
   - Purpose: Handles variations, approvals, conditional logic, and exceptions.

4. Output
   - Definition: The final deliverable, completed state, or terminal event of the flow.
   - Purpose: Signals successful or expected conclusion of the workflow cycle.
